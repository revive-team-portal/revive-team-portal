How Time Clock uses your data

What we collect. When you clock in, out, or take a break we record the time, the area you selected, a photo (selfie) taken at that moment, and your phone’s location.

Why. The photo confirms it’s really you (it stops someone clocking in for you) and the location confirms you’re at the cafe. Together they keep everyone’s hours accurate and fair.

Who sees it. Only your supervisor can view the photos and location, alongside your timesheet.

How long. Photos are automatically deleted after {{ settings.selfie_retention_days || 90 }} days. Your hours are kept for payroll.

Face matching. We do not run facial-recognition matching — the photo is simply stored against the punch for a human to see if needed.

If you decline location. Your punch is still recorded; it just notes that no location was shared.
